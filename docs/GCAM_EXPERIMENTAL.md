# Flame Google Camera experiment promoted to stable

The maintainer confirmed GCam works on Flame on **2026-10-17** and approved
promotion to the stable device/vendor `infinity-4.1-flame` branches. The Flame
local manifest's `main` branch selects them. Google Camera 8.7 remains bundled,
and Aperture is omitted.

Follow [GOOGLE_CAMERA.md](GOOGLE_CAMERA.md) for switching an existing experimental
checkout, making a fresh stable checkout, building and regression checks.
The original `gcam-experimental-flame` branches remain available.
